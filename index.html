How to play

  1. 🏹 Move with ◀ ▶, throw the spear with THROW (keyboard: ← → and Space).
  2. 🦇 Bats carry syntax boxes. Hit one to absorb the code.
  3. ⏸ The action pauses for a learning checkpoint question.
  4. ⚡ Answer correctly to earn an upgrade and ◈ points. Answer wrong and you still get beginner-friendly feedback.
  5. 👑 Clear every checkpoint and the Boss bat appears. Beat it to unlock the next language module.
  6. 🔰 Buffs you earn are permanent: a retry keeps every level you have built up.
  7. 🛍 Spend ◈ points in the Shop on spears, garments and particle effects.
  8. ⏯ Pause with ❚❚ (or Esc), and mute the sound with SFX.
